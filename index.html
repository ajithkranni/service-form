Register as an approved contractor

Complete this form to register your business with My Company and declare your workplace health and safety arrangements. We use this information to assess and onboard contractors before work begins on site.

Before you start

  • This form takes about 5 minutes. You can't save and return to it later, so have your ABN and (if applicable) a copy of your health and safety policy ready.
  • Fields marked * are mandatory. All other fields are optional but help us process your registration faster.
  • Some questions reveal extra fields depending on how you answer — for example, if you answer "Yes" to having a health and safety policy, you'll be asked to attach it.
  • You'll need to complete a short verification step before submitting.
